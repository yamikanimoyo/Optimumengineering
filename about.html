Our Story

A consulting & contracting partner across the full engineering spectrum

Optimum Engineering is a leading engineering services firm based in Johannesburg, South Africa. We provide expert engineering solutions, both consulting and contracting, across the broad spectrum of electrical, mechanical, civil, structural and architectural engineering, as well as project management and quantity surveying.

Optimum was founded in 2016 as a private company with a deliberate mission: to be an engineering firm structured in a way that affords personal involvement from its directors and associates, not only on every project but in direct personal contact with every client. We believe that structure is what lets us deliver a dynamic, accountable service, with the assurance that your interests are looked after by the people who run the company.

Our team of registered professional engineers, technologists, technicians, draughtsmen and project managers is dedicated to delivering innovative, efficient and cost-effective solutions tailored to each client's specific needs.
