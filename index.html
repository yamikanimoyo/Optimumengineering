Who We Are

A multidisciplinary engineering partner, built around people

Optimum Engineering is a leading engineering services firm based in Johannesburg, South Africa, providing expert consulting and contracting solutions across electrical, mechanical, civil, structural, architectural, project management and quantity surveying disciplines.

We were founded in 2016 and structured deliberately so our directors stay personally involved on every project and in direct contact with every client, giving you the assurance that your interests are looked after by the people who run the firm, not a layer removed from it.
